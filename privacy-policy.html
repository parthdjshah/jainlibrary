Privacy Policy

Effective date: 24 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Parth Jain Books handles information when you use the optional Smart Annotation feature.

1. Smart Annotation

Smart Annotation is optional. It lets you underline, bold, italicise, and highlight selected text in supported book pages and synchronize those annotations with a Google Sheet chosen by you.

2. Google Sheets data

When you enable Smart Annotation, Google may ask you to authorize access to your selected Google Sheet. The application uses that access only to read, create, update, and remove annotation records needed to provide the Smart Annotation feature requested by you.

The application does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or use it for unrelated purposes.

3. Annotation information

Annotation records may contain the book/page name, selected text, surrounding text used to relocate the selection, document-position information, annotation formatting, and timestamps. These records are stored in the Google Sheet you choose.

4. Browser storage

The website may store your Smart Annotation preference and the identifier of your selected spreadsheet in browser local storage. Your Google password is never requested by this website.

5. Sharing

We do not sell or rent your information. Google processes information according to Google's applicable policies and the permissions you grant through Google's authorization screen.

6. Retention and deletion

Annotations remain in your Google Sheet until you edit or delete them. You can also revoke the application's Google authorization through your Google Account settings.

7. Security

Google's authorization mechanisms are used for Google service access. We do not intentionally store Google API data on our server for unrelated purposes.

8. Changes

This policy may be updated when the website or Smart Annotation functionality changes. The revised effective date will be shown on this page.
