How to Use the AI Features in This Book

This book has built-in AI features that allow you to select a passage and ask questions about it, or open the AI assistant directly and ask your own question.

The AI feature prepares a prompt containing the book name, the book URL, the selected passage and, where available, the section/context of the passage. It then opens ChatGPT with that prompt.

1. Ask AI about a selected passage

Step 1 — Select text

Use your mouse on a computer, or touch and select text on a phone or tablet, to highlight the passage you want to ask about.

Step 2 — Open the AI menu

After you select text, the AI menu appears near the selected passage. The menu contains several actions.

The available actions

  • Explain — Ask ChatGPT to explain the selected passage.
  • Give example — Ask for an example that helps explain the selected passage.
  • Analyze — Ask ChatGPT to analyze the selected passage.
  • Why? — Ask about the reason or significance of what is stated in the passage.
  • Challenge the author — Ask a critical question or examine the author's statement from a questioning perspective.
  • Connect with earlier ideas — Ask ChatGPT to connect the selected passage with earlier ideas in the book.
  • Summarize — Ask for a concise summary of the selected passage.
  • Test me — Ask ChatGPT to test your understanding of the selected passage.
  • Ask my own question — Open the question box so you can type your own question.

2. Ask your own question

You can also use the floating 🤖 AI button on the page.

1. Click or tap the 🤖 button.

2. If you have selected a passage, it will be shown in the AI window.

3. Type your question in the question box.

4. Click Open in ChatGPT.

You can therefore use the AI feature even when you do not want to select a particular passage.

3. What information is sent to ChatGPT?

The AI feature prepares a prompt using information available on the current book page. The prompt includes:

  • The title of the current book/page.
  • The URL of the current book page.
  • The passage you selected, if any.
  • The section/context identified from the book, when available.
  • The action you selected or the question you typed.

The prompt also asks ChatGPT to answer primarily from the book and selected passage and not to invent information that is not supported by the text. Where the passage is ambiguous, ChatGPT is asked to say so.

Important: The AI feature opens ChatGPT with the prepared prompt. The quality of the answer depends on ChatGPT's response and on the information available in the book and selected passage. AI-generated explanations should therefore be checked against the original text when accuracy is important.

4. Examples of questions you can ask

After selecting a passage, you could ask:

Explain this passage in simple language.

What is the main idea of this passage?

Give me an example to understand this teaching.

How does this passage connect with an earlier idea in the book?

What question could I ask the author about this statement?

Test my understanding of this passage.

For Ask my own question, you can type any question you want into the question box.

5. Using the feature on a mobile phone

On a phone or tablet, press and hold on the text and use the normal text selection controls to highlight the passage. The AI selection menu should then appear.

You can also tap the floating 🤖 button to open the AI question window directly.

6. Using the feature on a computer

With a mouse, drag across the passage to select it. The AI menu will appear near the selected text. Choose the action you want.

Alternatively, click the floating 🤖 button to ask your own question.

7. Closing the AI window

When the AI question window is open, click Cancel to close it without opening ChatGPT.

8. A useful way to read the book with AI

A practical approach is to first read a passage yourself and then use the AI features to explore it further:

  1. Select a passage that you find interesting or difficult.
  2. Use Explain to understand it.
  3. Use Give example if the concept is difficult to visualize.
  4. Use Connect with earlier ideas to explore the book's internal connections.
  5. Use Challenge the author when you want to examine an argument critically.
  6. Use Test me to check your understanding.
  7. Use Ask my own question whenever you have a specific question.
The AI assistant is intended as a tool for exploring and understanding the book. It does not replace the original text.
